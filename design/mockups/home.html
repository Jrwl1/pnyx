What PNYX is — and isn't

PNYX is...

  • A public evidence tracker for political promises and party stances
  • Independent and nonpartisan — no editorial scores or ratings
  • Source-linked — every claim traces back to a public record
  • Party-aware — party stances tracked separately from individual promises, with party-line comparisons
  • Transparent about unknowns — missing data is labeled, not hidden

PNYX is not...

  • A partisan scorecard or rating system
  • An opinion or editorial platform
  • A substitute for official parliamentary records
  • A tool that infers fulfillment from incomplete data
  • A social media platform for political debate

Our methodology is public

Read how we define fulfillment status, vote alignment, party stances, and how we handle missing data. We believe the process matters as much as the results.
