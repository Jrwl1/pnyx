PNYX redesign plan

The goal is to stop showing backend objects directly and rebuild the public site around reader questions.

Working assumption

Design first for ordinary public readers. Keep audit-grade evidence one interaction away through tabs, drawers, and source lists.

1

Seed one real data slice

Five politicians, their parties, and roughly ten source-backed promises or positions. Enough real content to reveal design problems without pretending coverage is complete.

2

Redesign the main public journey

Home, politician profile, party profile, promise detail. Each page answers: who is this, what matters, what is proven, what is missing, and what can I do next?

3

Separate public reading from ops work

Public pages become calm, self-explanatory civic records. Ops pages remain dense review tools with tighter labels and fewer explanatory paragraphs.

4

Roll the design language across tabs

Tabs organize facts, promises, evidence, votes, discussion, and gaps. Backend fields stay available, but stop leading the experience.

What I will not do

No big fake launch dataset, no marketing-style hero, no card wall as the primary structure, no invented claims, no auto-published interpretations.
